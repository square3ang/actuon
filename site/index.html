Every song brings its own art.

Judged when you pressed. Not when the frame ran.

Notes run on a tick clock, apart from the frame rate. Windows and macOS time key presses on their own thread. Linux takes the compositor's timestamp, or the more exact kernel one with evdev on. None of them waits for the frame.
